Week2 : Superstore Sales Analysis

Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Sample Superstore dataset to identify factors affecting sales and profitability. The analysis explores business performance across different regions, customer segments, product categories, discounts, shipping methods, and relationships between key variables.

Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare performance across regions and identify growth trends.
- Evaluate customer segments and product categories.
- Investigate how discounts affect profitability.
- Analyze whether shipping factors influence performance.
- Identify relationships between sales, profit, quantity, and discount.

The dataset used is the Sample Superstore dataset, containing information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Regions
- Products
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discounts
- Profit
- Shipping details

Analysis Performed

Data Cleaning
- Checked dataset information and missing values.
- Converted order and shipping dates into datetime format.
- Created shipping duration features.

Exploratory Data Analysis
The following analyses were performed:

- Overall Sales and Profit Analysis
- Regional Performance Analysis
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Category Performance Analysis
- Discount Analysis
- Shipping Analysis
- Correlation Analysis

Key Findings

- The West region recorded the highest overall sales and profit, while the East region showed strong growth over time.
- Technology was the strongest-performing category based on sales and profit.
- Consumer customers contributed the highest sales and profit, while Home Office had the highest profit margin.
- Higher discount levels were associated with reduced profitability.
- Shipping factors showed less influence on profit compared to pricing and product-related factors.

Recommendations

- Review discount strategies to reduce losses from excessive discounts.
- Focus on profitable categories and customer segments.
- Continue monitoring regional growth opportunities.
- Consider profitability when making business decisions rather than focusing only on sales volume.
